# Arth: AI accountant for Indian small shops

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Arth keeps the books for a kirana or building-materials shop in plain chat, in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Tell it what happened at the counter and it records the khata entry, updates stock, makes GST bills, tracks who owes what and restocks from supplier agents. Nothing is written until you confirm it on a card.

## What you can say

- "Ramesh ko 10 bag cement becha 4200 mein, aadha cash mila"
- "Bill bana do Verma Constructions ke liye, 200 kg TMT 12mm"
- "Kis kis ka udhaar baaki hai?"
- "How much cement is left?"
- "Is mahine kitna profit hua?"

## What it does

- **Khata / ledger:** sales, purchases, expenses, money in and out, party balances
- **GST billing:** CGST/SGST or IGST split, invoice numbering, invoice cards
- **Inventory:** stock levels, low-stock alerts
- **Collections:** dues list, reminder messages with UPI payment links
- **Procurement:** quotes from supplier agents, negotiation, payment on the Fetch.ai testnet
- **Insights:** sales, profit and cash flow explained from your real numbers
