# Arth: AI accountant for Indian small shops

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Arth keeps the books for a kirana or building-materials shop in plain chat, in English, Hindi or Hinglish. Tell it what happened at the counter and it records the khata entry, updates stock, makes GST bills, tracks who owes what and restocks from supplier agents. Nothing is written until you confirm it on a card.

## What you can say

- "Ramesh ko 10 bag cement becha 4200 mein, aadha cash mila"
- "Bill bana do Verma Constructions ke liye, 200 kg TMT 12mm"
- "Kis kis ka udhaar baaki hai?" / "Who do I owe the most?" / "Ramesh se kitna lena hai?"
- "How much cement is left?" / "Cement ka rate kya hai?" / "2 bag cement kharab ho gaya"
- "Cement ka price 430 kar do" / "Naya customer Mohan Lal 9876543210"
- "Last bill dikhao" / "Bill 3 cancel karo"
- "Is mahine kitna profit hua?" / "Sabse zyada kya bikta hai?" / "Best customer kaun hai?"
- "Pichle mahine se sale badhi?" / "Is mahine GST kitna banega?" / "Kharcha kahan gaya?"
- "Is mahine ka average bill kitna hai?" / "Hafte ke kis din sabse zyada bikri hoti hai?" / any other question about your records

## What it does

- **Khata / ledger:** sales, purchases, expenses, money in and out, party balances and phone numbers, adding customers and suppliers
- **GST billing:** CGST/SGST or IGST split, invoice numbering, invoice cards, finding and cancelling past bills
- **Inventory:** stock levels, low-stock alerts, rates and margins, stock value, damaged or counted stock, adding and editing items
- **Collections:** dues list (who owes you, whom you owe, how long), reminder messages with UPI payment links
- **Procurement:** quotes from supplier agents, negotiation, payment on the Fetch.ai testnet
- **Insights:** sales, profit, best items and customers, month-on-month comparison, GST estimate, expenses by category and supplier rates, explained from your real numbers
